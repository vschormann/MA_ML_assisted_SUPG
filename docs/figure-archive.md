# Thesis figure archive

The PNG files under `docs/assets/thesis-figures/` were historical root-level
exports from the submitted research workflow. They are retained as figure
artefacts and do not represent new results from the refactoring.

The `Ex*` files record comparisons from Chapter 4. Files with `BJK`,
`perturbed`, or `revised` in their names belong to the revised Chapter 5 study.
The landing page uses `revised_results_lineplots.png` as a representative
Chapter 5 comparison.

The remaining files are preserved for reference and can be included in the
deterministic figure-rendering workflow when their provenance is documented.
